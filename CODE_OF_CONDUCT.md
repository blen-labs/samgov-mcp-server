# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Be constructive when disagreeing, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the [BLEN Labs organization](https://github.com/blen-labs). Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.
